I built a tool for my own job hunt, because most of applying was typing the same answers into the same forms.

Today it's open source: **LinKit All**.

It runs on your own computer and:
→ finds jobs on 295 sources (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, LinkedIn and Indeed listings, regional boards, any careers page)
→ ranks them against your CV, locally, with no AI cost
→ tailors your CV a few words per job, with rules that refuse invented employers, dates or numbers
→ fills in the applications, and leaves visa, salary and similar questions to you

It starts in Practice mode: it fills every form but never presses Submit.

You bring your own AI: a ChatGPT or Claude plan, an API key, or a local model with Ollama.

The honest number: on 115 real job postings in Practice mode, 67 of the 70 it may finish on its own (96%) got as far as Practice mode allows. Small sample, nothing submitted, and the full scorecard is public.

Free, AGPL-3.0, Windows / macOS / Linux, English and Arabic.

👉 https://github.com/silentco2/LinKit-All

If it saves you an application, a ⭐ on GitHub helps other job seekers find it.
