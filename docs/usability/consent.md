# Thank you for helping us

We are trying out an app called **LinKit All**. It finds jobs, adjusts your CV and fills in applications. We want to see where it is hard to use, so we can make it easier.

**We are testing the app, not you.** You cannot do anything wrong.

## What will happen

- The session takes about 45 minutes. You will do a few small tasks and think out loud while you do them.
- Everything runs in **practice mode**. No application is sent to any company during the session.
- A week later we may ask you for a second, 15-minute session. You can say no.

## Recording (your choice)

- We would like to record your **screen and your voice**, so we do not miss anything you say. This is optional. You can say no and we will take notes instead.
- Recordings stay on this computer. They are never uploaded or shared. We delete them once our notes are written, and in any case within 30 days.

## Your information

- All of your data stays on this computer. LinKit All has no tracking.
- If you use your own CV, its text is sent to the AI service connected to the app for this session, as the app normally works. If you are not comfortable with that, you can use a sample CV that we provide. It is a made-up person.
- Please do not type any real passwords, bank details or ID numbers. If the app asks for a passphrase, make one up for today.
- **Your CV is deleted from this computer when the session ends.** We delete the app's data for your session at the same time.
- In our notes we use a code (like P3) instead of your name. We may quote your words, without your name.

## You are in control

- You can **stop at any time**, for any reason, without explaining.
- You can skip any question.
- You can ask us to delete your notes and recording later by telling us your code.
