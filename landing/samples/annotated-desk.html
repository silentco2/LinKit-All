What it will not do

  • Send your data anywhere but your AI.It runs on your computer with no account and no telemetry. Only the AI you pick sees CV and job text.
  • Invent anything on your CV.Employers, titles, dates and numbers are locked. Every edit is checked by rules before it is kept.
  • Guess your sensitive answers.Work authorization, visa, salary, demographics, disability, veteran status and criminal record come from your profile or wait for you.
  • Press Submit in Practice mode.It fills the forms and stops. Applying for real is a switch you turn on.
  • Call it applied without proof.A confirmation page or email is needed. Otherwise the job is marked failed, with the reason.
  • Drive LinkedIn, Indeed or Glassdoor.A browser extension fills the form in your own browser, and you press Submit.
