Everything before the application

Five steps, each one runnable on its own. Open a folder tab.

Find

One search across public job APIs, job boards, regional sites and any careers page you paste. Duplicates are merged, and each site is paced so it is not hammered.

  • Greenhouse
  • Lever
  • Ashby
  • Workday
  • 100+ more job APIs
  • LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor via JobSpy
  • ZipRecruiter, Google, Bayt
  • Wuzzuf, Forasna
  • Any careers page
  • LinkedIn hiring posts
  • Telegram channels

LinkedIn hiring posts and Telegram channels are opt-in.

Check

Offline scam and ghost-job scores, in English and Arabic, with no AI call. Likely scams are dropped. Likely ghost jobs rank low.

Scam signs: fees, contact moved off the platform, crypto or ID requests, a domain that does not match.

Ghost signs: old postings, repeat sightings across runs, vague evergreen text.

Rank

A free local model compares your CV with each job. Then your AI scores the best ones, in batches. The cut-offs are yours to change.

  • Strong matchApplied automatically
  • Possible matchYou approve first
  • Weak matchHidden

Tailor

A few words change per job, never a rewrite. Layout, employers, titles, dates and numbers never change, and rules check every edit. Word, LaTeX, ODT and PDF CVs are supported. If the result does not match the original layout, the edits are rolled back.

Scan one QR code on your PC and your phone is paired. No account, no app store: it is a web app you add to your Home Screen, on Android and iPhone.

Why not a LinkedIn Easy Apply bot?

Bots that click Easy Apply on LinkedIn put your account at risk and send the same CV everywhere. LinKit All never drives LinkedIn. It ranks jobs against your CV first, tailors the CV a few words per job, applies on company sites like Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday, and leaves visa, salary and similar questions to you.

Questions

Is it safe to let it apply for me?

It starts in Practice mode: it fills every form and takes screenshots but never presses Submit. Applying for real is a switch you turn on, and each submit waits for your OK unless you turn that off. Your data stays in one folder on your computer, and saved passwords are in an encrypted vault.

Does it work on LinkedIn?

It finds LinkedIn, Indeed and Glassdoor listings, but it never drives those sites, because automated applying gets accounts banned there. The LinKit Fill browser extension fills their forms in your own browser, and you press Submit.

Which AI does it need?

The one you already have: a ChatGPT plan through the Codex CLI, a Claude plan through the Claude Code CLI, an API key, or a local model through Ollama or LM Studio. With a local model, your CV and job text stay on your computer.

Is it free?

Yes. LinKit All is free and open source under AGPL-3.0, with no account. AI use counts against your own plan or API key; a local model costs nothing extra.

Will it lie on my CV?

It is built not to. It changes a few words per job and keeps your layout, and rules refuse edits that change employers, titles, dates, degrees, numbers or contact details. In the project's own test, 17 of 19 planted dishonest edits were refused, and you can read every change in a red-line view before anything is sent.

Install

    uv tool install linkit-all
  2. Run linkit. The app opens in your browser.
  3. Upload your CV, pick your AI, then press Find jobs in practice mode.
